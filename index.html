About

I am Dean, Faculty of Computing at the Lahore University of Biological and Applied Sciences, where I lead the faculty's academic direction, research and industry engagement. I have more than 23 years of experience across universities and industry, including a tenured professorship at COMSATS University Islamabad and leadership of product and machine learning teams at Persivia.

My research covers computer vision, medical imaging, natural language processing, biometrics, video analytics and large language models. I lead the Machine Perception and Visual Intelligence Research Group, and my work has been supported by more than 49 million Pakistani rupees in research funding.

I am a Certified Entrepreneurial Coach, a Program Evaluator for the National Computing Education Accreditation Council, and a Director of the Artificial Intelligence Education Foundation. I have co-founded two technology companies, and outside work I enjoy philately, numismatics, swimming, trekking and travel.

Research

My group works at the point where machines learn to see, read and reason. Current and past work spans the following areas.

Computer vision and video analytics

Surveillance analytics, anomaly recognition, fire and smoke detection, and multi camera tracking.

Medical image processing

Brain tumor analysis from magnetic resonance images, lung nodule detection, and COVID 19 detection.

Biometrics and information security

Face, fingerprint and iris recognition, and multi biometric access control.

Natural language processing

Language processing for Urdu, including sentiment analysis, spelling correction and named entity recognition.

Large language models

Retrieval augmented chatbots and clinical data extraction in healthcare settings.

Agri informatics

Weed and crop classification and plant disease detection.

Research group: Machine Perception and Visual Intelligence Research Group
